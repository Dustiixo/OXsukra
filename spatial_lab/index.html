HOW THIS IS GROUNDED

Pretty is not proof.

The central world is an experimental three-dimensional *projection* of real FlowMesh branch relations, verified excerpt events and their shared sources. It is not a recording of hidden AI thoughts.

Shapes, particles, ambience and motion are visual design. Branch creation, parking, evidence links, text, hashes and timeline markers come from the loaded trace.

This demo comes from a verified local FlowMesh run. You may load a private trace JSON exported with spatial_lab/export_trace.py. The viewer makes no network requests when opened as a file. An optional local-only live bridge is supplied separately.

For privacy, do not publish personal traces: they can include research quotations and project names.
