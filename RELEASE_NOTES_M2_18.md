# VELA M2.18 — queued musical response audition export

Native Tk control **EXPORT QUEUED AUDITION .WAV** saves an offline WAV containing the current mix and already-approved response at its scheduled phrase boundary. Requires an approved queued response, renders at least one loop and through the entry plus one bar (up to 120 seconds), and never approves proposals or advances live transport. The standard mix exporter and FID format are unchanged.
